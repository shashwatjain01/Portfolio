01 / MOTIVATION

Why this problem?

Regular, approachable practice can make learning mathematics easier to sustain. MathQuest organizes practice around school grades and challenges.

02 / THE NEED

What needed to be addressed.

Give learners in grades 5–8 a clear place to find practice material, attempt challenges, and request help or share feedback.

03 / THE SOLUTION

How it was approached.

  • Grade-based sections and a challenge interface.
  • Practice-sheet navigation and a request/feedback hub.
  • Responsive HTML, CSS, and JavaScript frontend.

04 / OUTCOME & CONTEXT

Grades 5–8

A web learning project with grade-based practice content and weekly challenge features.
